## NASA Scientific Visualization Studio — CGI Moon Kit (SVS 4720)

Files: `moon_albedo_2k.png`, `moon_albedo_4k.png`, `moon_albedo_8k.png`,
`moon_normal_2k.png`, `moon_normal_4k.png`

* Source page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720 (downloaded 2026-10-24)
* Inputs:
  * `lroc_color_poles_2k.tif`, `lroc_color_poles_4k.tif`, `lroc_color_poles_8k.tif` —
    colour mosaic from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) Wide Angle Camera,
    2048×1024 / 4096×2048 / 8192×4096.
  * `ldem_16_uint.tif` — LRO Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) elevation, 5760×2880
    (16 pixels per degree), unsigned 16-bit in half-metre units.
* Credit requested by the page: **"NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio"**.
* Terms: the SVS help page states that SVS content "is in the public domain (unless otherwise
  noted), meaning that it is free to download, use, and redistribute for whatever purposes you
  see fit". No exception is noted on the CGI Moon Kit page. Use of NASA imagery does not imply
  NASA endorsement; the pack does not use NASA logos or insignia.
* Processing (`tools/prep_moon.py`, reproducible): sRGB → linear, area-weighted mean albedo
  normalised to 0.28, linear-space box-filtered mip pyramid packed into a 1.5W×H atlas;
  normals from central differences of the LOLA DEM (resampled to 2048/4096 px, true
  metric pixel spacing including the cos(latitude) term).
* Honesty note: the "8K" option uses the native 8192×4096 colour mosaic. No upscaled data is
  labelled as higher resolution; the relief map stops at 4K because the DEM is 5760 px wide.

## NASA SVS Deep Star Maps 2020 (SVS 4851) - contains ESA Gaia data

File: `milkyway.png` (used when **NASA Milky Way** is on, the default)

* Source page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851 (downloaded 2026-10-24), input
  `milkyway_2020_4k.exr` - the Milky Way and faint stars in celestial coordinates (plate
  carrée, linear half-float), with the bright Hipparcos/Tycho stars removed.
* Credit requested by the page: **NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization
  Studio**; **Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC**.
* Terms - read carefully: SVS content is public domain "unless otherwise noted", but this map
  is built from Gaia DR2 data, and ESA's Gaia data licence page states "Gaia data are
  distributed under the CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO license"
  (https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia-users/license). The conservative reading, adopted
  here: treat `milkyway.png` as **CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO - attribution required, no commercial
  use**. The pack's code stays MIT. Distributing the pack where it earns money (paid
  downloads, platform revenue sharing) may conflict with this; to avoid the question, delete
  `milkyway.png` and its `customTexture.milkyWayTex` line and set the option off (the
  procedural Milky Way remains). Not legal advice.
* Processing (`tools/prep_milkyway.py`, reproducible): 2x2 box filter to 2048×1024 in linear
  space, stored as 8-bit with a cube-root curve. Orientation verified from the data (galactic
  centre and Large Magellanic Cloud positions), not assumed.

## Published methods and data used as facts (not code)

These are physical constants or published mathematics implemented independently:

* LabPBR Material Standard 1.3 channel layout and the complex refractive indices (n, k) of its
  eight predefined metals (shaderlabs.org wiki).
* Earth-atmosphere scattering coefficients (Rayleigh at 680/550/440 nm, continental aerosol,
  ozone Chappuis band) as tabulated in the atmospheric-rendering literature.
* Pure-water absorption at visible wavelengths; water's refractive index dispersion
  (1.3311 / 1.3345 / 1.3395 at 650 / 550 / 450 nm).
* `hashU` in `lib/core/noise.glsl` is the "lowbias32" integer mixer published into the public
  domain by Chris Wellons; the R2 low-discrepancy sequence is from Martin Roberts (2018);
  interleaved gradient noise is from Jorge Jimenez (2014). All are short formulas, credited
  in place.
